Three planets. Three ways to disappear.

Planet 7518 — "Planet 7"

Vertical gardens, orderly cities

A stunning green planet of vertical gardens, modern buildings, and orderly cities. Known for structure, work, and productivity, it manufactures most of the interplanetary goods worth having. Free healthcare, a fully green ecosystem with no synthetics, and great jobs — even for Planet 1 speakers. Live the calm life where you belong.

Planet 6181 — "Planet 6"

Sprawling vineyards, endless food

Sunny, sprawling vineyards, beautiful people and did we mention the food? Who could ask for more? Come to Planet 6 if you're ready for the luxury life without poor people to ruin it.

Planet 1016 — "Planet 10"

Technofuture

Neon lights, flying cars, and wild technology have taken over the planet, and it's what you've always dreamed of. Get everything you need generated just for you. Is it fake? Who cares. Live inside your virtual world, forever. Cyborg women companions so you never have to be alone.

Rai & Logan // outside the Disintegration Zone

It's 2060 and the tech bros have taken over the planet. Rai, a cyberpunk girl from Planet 1, decides to ghost her planet, her boyfriend, and the life she knows forever. She's in search of a greener pasture, a quieter life that her "what planet are you?" quiz promised: Planet 7518.

What she quickly learns is that not everything is what it seems, and integrating into another galaxy is so soul-crushing, she must find a way out. The Disintegration Zone is a glass dome utopia on Planet 7 that promises a perfect life.

That's when she meets Logan.

Now, it's too late — and even though she tries to stay friends, she can't stop herself from falling for him. But it may be too late for them.
