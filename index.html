Rai escapes from her tech bro-pocalypse planet to find a better life on an alternative planet where technology is old and nature still thrives. When integrating into this alien planet feels impossible, she learns of the Disintegration Zone, a utopia bubble sealed off from “the others” and signs up without hesitation.

Then she starts falling for Logan.

Now she must decide: leave him behind for paradise, or risk her one shot at utopia to stay.
